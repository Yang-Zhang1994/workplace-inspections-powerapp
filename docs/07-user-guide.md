# Inspector quick guide

1. Open **Power Apps** on your phone → *Workplace Inspections*.
2. **+ New** → pick the **group, building and room** (each list narrows the next).
3. Enter the date, what you found, the **severity** and hazard type.
4. **High** severity: write the corrective action and a due date within 7 days — the app won't save without them, and the safety lead is emailed automatically.
5. **Attach file → Take photo** to add up to 5 photos.
6. **Save.** The finding appears at the top of the list.
7. To update a finding, select it → **Edit** (pencil). When it's fixed, set **Status** to *Closed* and save.

Red due dates are overdue. Overdue findings are also emailed once a day.
